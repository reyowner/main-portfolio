- **Black Rose emblems:** supplied by the user from their Black Rose project. Original files are unchanged. Web copies in `public/images/` trim transparent padding and fit within 768 × 768 pixels. The white emblem is displayed in the portfolio; the black variant is retained for light-surface use.
- **Technology glyphs:** selected exports from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/), distributed under CC0 1.0. The installed package's full license is in `node_modules/simple-icons/LICENSE.md`. Brand names and marks retain their respective owners' rights. Icons are monochrome to fit the requested palette. No Flaticon assets are used.
- **Fonts:** Sora and Instrument Sans, locally hosted through Fontsource packages; see each package's bundled license.
- **21st.dev references:** Spline Scene, Sonar Grid, and Agentic Factory 3D were reviewed for layout and interaction inspiration. No component code, external Spline scene, screenshot, or model was copied.
- **Room screen graphics and hologram:** original procedural illustrations. Screens are visual interpretations of the projects, not screenshots of their deployed interfaces.
- **Live destinations:** https://predikta.ai/ and https://blackrose.asia/ were supplied by the user and inspected during the revision.

- **PREDIKTA mark:** supplied user asset copied byte-for-byte from `netopia-predikta/frontend/public/new-version-assets/predikta-signup.png`; displayed in collection, detail, and monitor.
- **Client portfolio marks:** original SVG monogram (Jervin) and open-book icon (Ria), authored for this portfolio. No third-party icon asset is used for either.
- **Client project links:** https://habagat-jervin-eport.vercel.app/ and https://portfolio-ria-rho.vercel.app/ supplied by the user. Ria project description and technology list were checked against its local source.
- **Résumé:** supplied RReoner_CV.pdf copied unchanged for the explicitly requested public download.
